    FAQ

    Good questions first

    Is there a paid membership or checkout?

    No. Community membership is free, and this site does not collect payments. Organizational and project partnership scopes are discussed before any commitment.

    Are the certificates third-party carbon credits?

    No. They are signed records generated by Terra Nova from user-provided footprint estimates; they are not offsets, credits, or an independent emissions audit.

    How do I propose a project partnership?

    Choose Project partner below, share your organization and project context, and the request will be recorded for administrator follow-up.

    What happens after I send the contact form?

    Your request is stored with a reference number and appears in the admin inquiry queue. The team can review it, reply to your email, and update its follow-up status.
